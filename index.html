Alfred Google Workspace

A personal-use Google Workspace integration for the account owner's AI assistant, Alfred.

The Google OAuth consent screen currently identifies this app as gws CLI. This website provides its app information, privacy notice and terms. It is not a public sign-in service.

What the integration does

Alfred connects to the owner's Google account to carry out requested assistance: finding and summarising email, preparing and sending approved messages, managing files and documents, organising calendars, working with spreadsheets and looking up contacts.

Google account authorisation enables API access; it does not by itself approve every action. Sending, sharing, deletion and other consequential operations require the owner's specific instruction.

Google permissions

  • Gmail: read messages and settings, organise messages, and compose and send email.
  • Drive, Docs and Sheets: read, create, edit and delete files, documents and spreadsheets available to the authorised account.
  • Calendar: read, edit, share and delete accessible calendars and their events.
  • Contacts: read and download contacts; no contact editing permission.

Access is limited to the Google account and permissions explicitly authorised through Google. This information website does not receive OAuth credentials or Google account content.

Personal use only

The integration is maintained for the owner's personal use. There is no public registration, subscription, payment or invitation to connect other Google accounts. It is not a Google product and is not endorsed by Google.
